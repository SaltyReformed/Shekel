The arms read as the value's own phrase ("every 14 days", "monthly on day 15", "twice a month on days 1 and 15"). ONE macro on all four doors -- registration, the generate card and its standalone page, regenerate and reset -- so the wording, the ``name=`` attributes and the ``value=`` tokens cannot drift between the door that sets a rhythm and the door that corrects it; the same discipline render_select + PAYDAY_SHIFT_OPTIONS keep for the convention.
