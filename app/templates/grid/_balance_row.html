Ruling R-O seats the whole "how this balance is reached" chain here, in one visual block: Timing & true-ups, then Contributions, then the modelled return, then Projected End Balance. Reading top to bottom from the Net Cash Flow row above, that is the identity balance[p] - balance[p-1] == net[p] + reconciliation[p] + contribution[p] + accrual[p] and it holds because all five figures are one GridColumn -- one valued row set grouped two ways plus its two modelled tiers -- rather than separate producers a test keeps in step (ruling R-K). ROW ORDER is the MODEL's own (ruling R-AH): a contribution lands on its pay period's start_date and the day's accrual is then taken on the balance that day ENDS holding, so the money is contributed and then earns. Addition is commutative and the identity holds either way; reading order is not.
