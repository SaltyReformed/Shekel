{% from "_money_macros.html" import money %} {# The balance-write acknowledgement (plan step X-f1e3, finding N-199; re-keyed at plan step X-f2-b, finding N-204). Swapped out-of-band into ``#anchor-ack-mount`` (``base.html``) by the anchor true-up's success response and auto-shown by the ``data-toast-auto-show`` handler in ``app/static/js/app.js``. The mount is the toast twin of ``#modal-mount``, and it exists for the same stated reason: the anchor editor opens from FIVE surfaces (the grid cell, the dashboard hero, the cockpit card cell, the investment hero, the cash hero) and an out-of-band swap needs a target that exists on all of them. **Why an acknowledgement exists at all.** When a write leaves the GOVERNING balance where it was, the cell re-renders from ``cash_ledger.resolve_anchor`` to the same figure it already showed, and the response is indistinguishable from having done nothing -- so the user's next move is to enter it again. That is the same defect ``_anchor_editor_error`` prevents on the failure side. A back-dated assertion is the obvious case (a later reading still governs), and finding N-204 is the one that was missed: re-recording the balance that already governs for a LATER day writes a row, moves the coverage boundary, and changes nothing on screen. **Why it is not the ``#anchor-as-of`` caption it used to ride.** Two different facts were sharing one element: "as of " captions the assertion that governs NOW (durable, per-surface), while this acknowledges the REQUEST just made (transient, and often about a day that is not the governing one). Riding the caption gave the transient fact the durable one's mounting problem -- skipped outright on three surfaces, and destroyed on the dashboard by the very ``balanceChanged`` refresh this response triggers (finding N-199). **It APPENDS rather than replacing its mount** (finding N-206). The swap was ``hx-swap-oob="true"`` on an element carrying the mount's own id, which is an outerHTML swap: a second back-dated save inside the 8s autohide window replaced the mount and the first toast was simply lost. ``beforeend:`` appends instead, and ``app.js`` disposes each toast on ``hidden.bs.toast`` so the mount does not accumulate detached instances with armed timers. The wrapper below is load-bearing for that: for any swap style OTHER than outerHTML, htmx moves the CHILD NODES of the annotated element into the target (``htmx.min.js``, the ``He``/``a`` pair), so annotating the toast itself would append its innards and drop the ``.toast`` element. Expected context: account -- the Account whose balance was asserted. balance -- Decimal, the balance this submission ASSERTED (not necessarily the one that governs). observed_on -- the civil day it was asserted for. was_written -- True when a row was appended (``COMMITTED``), False when the submission matched the governing assertion and ruling R-EQ rolled it back. The copy has to say which: a toast reading "Balance recorded" over a write that wrote nothing is the same class of untruth this acknowledgement exists to remove. #}
