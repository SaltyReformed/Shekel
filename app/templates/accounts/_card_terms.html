{# The card's terms (plan step credit_card:CC-2; developer ruling R-CC25, 2026-09-18): the parameters card a revolving account shows below its balance band on the cash detail page, exactly where an interest-bearing account shows its APY card. Blank until the owner saves the card's terms, filled from the row after; until then the card is a DORMANT plain liability (design 3.4: no auto-create) and no card feature is offered. The cockpit plan step CC-11 builds replaces this page for a card and carries this form on. Every control renders every time, so a submit is the WHOLE row and the one door it posts to (card.save_terms, ruling R-CC24) creates or rewrites it; the five NOT NULL terms are required controls (cash back pre-filled 0.00 for a card with no terms), the two nullable ones may be left blank. Rates are entered as percents and stored as fractions (E-28): the schema divides, the template multiplies back through |to_percent. Below the terms, once they exist: the card's APR (plan step credit_card:CC-3; developer rulings R-CC27 and R-CC28, 2026-09-18) -- the rate in effect today, the dated series newest first with a remove button per row, and a two-control form that SETS the APR for a date (a date already listed is rewritten, so a same-date resubmit is the correction). Nothing renders here while the card is dormant: every card feature gates on the terms row (design 3.4). Context: account, card_terms (a CreditCardParams row, or None), card_aprs (CardApr rows, newest first; empty for a dormant card), card_apr_today (the fraction in effect on the page's day, or None). #}
