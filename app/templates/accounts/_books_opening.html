Balances you recorded after this day are left exactly as they are, so the difference shows up as a correction against the first of them. On a savings, investment or property account that correction is reported as a gain or as interest -- so a large restatement can briefly show a return that never happened. The balance stays right. Restating those later balances too is what clears it.
