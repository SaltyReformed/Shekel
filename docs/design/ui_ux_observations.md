# UI/UX Overhaul

## Overall

- Some of the muted gray text is difficult to read.
- Some text is small and difficult to read.
- Some muted gray buttons are difficult to see and recognize as a button.
- The Shekel name with the coin logo needs a unique font.
- The bright white and the off-white background colors in light mode are inconsistently applied and
  blend in with the gray text
- Are breadcrumbs necessary? Most pages are on the navbar.

## Dashboard

- Does it need the "Dashboard" breadcrumb at the top of the page?
- Text is cramped on the expenses due timeline
- The divider between the end balance chart and the expenses due timeline is unnecessary

## Grid

- No alternating contrasting rows. This might make the page too busy but it is hard to follow the
  row all the way across the page.
- Categories have a parent/child relationship that is invisible on the grid. Only the parent is
  visible. The child category is only visible when adding a new transaction.
- The Projected End Balance row header is cut off so that only "Projected E..." is visible.
- The grid doesn't have rounded corners but everything else does.

## Recurring

- The columns don't align between Income, Expenses, and Transfers
- The Monthly side of the Monthly/Per Paycheck selector is square while every other button is
  rounded.
- The totals on the Income, Expenses, and Transfers headers blend in with the column headers and
  buttons beneath them.

## Accounts

- Too many cards. The page looks busy.
- Difficult to distinguish between sections for Asset, Liability, Retirement, etc.
- The bar in the net worth hero has a legend with Asset, Retirement, and Liability but there is a
  section of the bar that doesn't have a legend item.

## Salary

- COLA and Merit raise banners and badges only need to display on the first paycheck not every one
  that month.
- Edit Salary Profile page needs updating to the new theme.
- The Salary Path - raises applied card needs labels for the x-axis and y-axis. The line doesn't
  make sense unlabeled.

## Retirement

- Pension Profiles page needs updating to the new theme.
- The shades of blue in the bar for the Income in Retirement card are too close in color.

## Analytics

- Unnecessary open space at the top of the page.
- The bright blue for the tabs doesn't seem consistent with the color palette.
- The tabs and the tab header seem redundant.
- The tabs don't feel like tabs. They look more like buttons. It's not immediately obvious that
  clicking Taxes will open a new Analytics tab.

### Calendar

- The calendar tab seems like it is almost the cockpit look of the other pages but not quite.
- The CSV export functionality can be removed root and branch.

### Spending

- The Spending tab doesn't quite fit the cockpit look of the other pages.
- The Where It Went card is disjointed. There are long bars and then a lot of data clustered at the
  end (sparklines, trend, amount, percentage)
- The Top Movers card and the Where It Went card seem like they could be merged into a more cohesive
  visual.
- There is a lot of text and numbers when it seems like the same data could be presented in one or
  more charts better.

### Statements

- The Statements tab could use some color or something to distinguish Assets from Liabilities from
  Equity etc. Maybe something like the Recurring page?

### Taxes

- Could the Taxes tab include a chart with levers similar to other pages like /retirement and loan
  details? It would give it a more consistent look.

## Settings

- Settings needs to updating to the new theme.
- The layout is inconsistent with the rest of the app.
