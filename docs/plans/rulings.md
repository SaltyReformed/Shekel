# The rulings

**Every developer ruling of every arc that has moved here, one line each: the RULE, not the
deliberation.** The deliberation belongs in the commit that shipped it, and a ruling whose work has
fully shipped is in the as-built record of the step that shipped it.

**The key is `(arc, id)` and the arc is a COLUMN, never a prefix** (conventions.md rule 10). Two
arcs may hold the same bare id: `balance:R-GU` and `bank_import:R-GU` are two different rulings and
both are LEGAL here. What that costs is a BARE citation of such an id resolving to two rules, so
**a citation must name its arc wherever the id is ambiguous**. No gate grades that; the writer does.

**Why the tables moved.** Ruling ids came from ONE global sequence spelled across five arc documents
in THREE grammars, and `tools/plan_gate` parsed none of them, so a collision was invisible to every
gate: `R-EX` named two rulings in 2026-08-10, and on 2026-08-27 `R-GW` was minted in both `balance`
and `bank_import` on two unmerged branches by sessions that had each checked --
**a `docs/` grep cannot see an unmerged branch.** Holding the pair here makes such a collision legal
and gradeable rather than silent. The lift itself is as-built at `b8f1c862` and `91f95f43`.

**The ruling registry stands at 269 rows.**
