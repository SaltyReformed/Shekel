# The findings ledger

**Every open finding in every arc, in ONE table, and every row names a LIVE owner.** A row leaves
when its fix SHIPS, not when a ruling settles it: a decided-but-unbuilt defect is still ahead of
you. Closed rows move to their arc's as-built record under the same id.

| column | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `arc` + `id` | The key. An `id` alone is not unique across the corpus -- `D4` names three different findings -- so cite both |
| `also` | `= arc:id` is the SAME claim, merged into this row. `~ arc:id` is a DISTINCT finding sharing a root cause. **The two are not interchangeable and conflating them deletes work** |
| `finding (one line)` | The defect, within a per-row cap. If it needs more, it belongs in the owning step's specification |
| `worst measured` | What it costs, measured rather than estimated |
| `status` | What is ruled and what is still owed |
| `owner` | An unticked `steps.md` id, or `operator`, or `developer-decision` |

**The rules are `conventions.md`**: rule 1 on owners, rule 10 on the key, and "The two relations in
`ledger.md`" on the `also` column. The steps the owners name are `steps.md`; each arc's argument
stays in its own document.
